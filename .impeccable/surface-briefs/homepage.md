# Static homepage

Target: astro/src/pages/index.astro
Mode: Persuade

## Direction contract

**THESIS:** A premium editorial presentation of the existing Growtox offer. The bottle is the visual signature; real client stories and a readable process earn the action.

**OWN-WORLD:** Coral-red fields, violet controls, cold white margins, near-black type. Montserrat with oversized tightly set headlines, generous whitespace, fine rules, rectangular image crops, and unboxed editorial content.

**STORY:** Understand the aesthetic-practice offer, read attributed results, understand the three-step process, recognize client practices, and qualify through the live form.

**FIRST VIEWPORT:** Slim white header. Large coral hero with dark stacked headline at left, a violet qualification button below, and original oversized bottle at right. A narrow bottom rule anchors a system link and scroll cue. On mobile, text leads, followed by the bottle; controls remain above the image.

**FORM:** Premium editorial, explicitly selected and pinned by the user in the approved plan. No random seed supersedes this choice. Code-first; no generated comp.

**FINISH:** unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance

## Motion thesis

Focal moment: the Growtox bottle settles into its hero composition while the heading reveals line by line, like an editorial cover assembling. Supporting copy and controls arrive within 700ms. Process rows enter as an ordered sequence; image and quote sections receive quieter one-time reveals. Controls use short color/arrow feedback. No perpetual motion, scroll hijacking, or animation-gated reading. Reduced motion removes spatial movement.

## Boundaries

All eight section groups in the approved plan remain. Live CTA links only. The case-study link opens the live homepage because its download is handled by a WordPress popup. No collection of visitor data locally.
